# AI Idol Design System — Apple Design Standard (White & Apple Green)

## Direction

Apple Human Interface Guidelines (macOS Sequoia / iOS 18) with a modern desktop studio layout: pure white surfaces and subtle canvas gray (`#F5F5F7`), translucent frosted glass chrome (`backdrop-filter: blur(25px)`), signature Apple Green accents (`#34C759`), refined continuous squircles, and layered soft shadows.

## Foundations

- Color: Apple Canvas (`#F5F5F7`), Pure White Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), Text Primary (`#1D1D1F`), Text Secondary (`#6E6E73`), Hairline Borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`), and Apple System Green (`#34C759`) for primary actions and active states.
- Type: SF Pro Text & Display (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "SF Pro Text"`).
- Spacing: 8pt Apple rhythm (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32px).
- Shape: 10px controls/inputs, full pill (9999px) for status chips & tags, 14–16px cards, 20–24px dialogs.
- Elevation: Multi-layered soft ambient shadows (`0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.03)`).
- Icons: Material Symbols Rounded with clean geometry.

## Components

- Buttons: Apple Green solid primary actions (`#34C759`, hover `#28CD41`, text white), Apple white card secondary actions with subtle hairline borders.
- Inputs: Crisp white inputs, subtle inset shadow, Apple Green focus ring (`outline: 3px solid rgba(52, 199, 89, 0.25)`).
- Monitor: Studio-grade dark video viewport with crisp Apple outer framing.
- Drawers & Sheets: Apple macOS Vibrancy with frosted blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)` + `blur(35px)`).

## UX Writing

- Verbs for actions: “Phát live”, “Lưu”, “Tải lên”, “Gửi”.
- Nouns for destinations: “Tổng quan”, “Avatar”, “Thư viện”, “Cài đặt”.
- Direct, friendly Vietnamese tone tailored for livestream operators.
